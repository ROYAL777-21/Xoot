# Project Context

## Company Mission

Xoot is a premium South African home-care platform that connects clients with vetted independent cleaning contractors called Xooters. The mission is to make home care feel reliable, remembered, protected, and handled without positioning the brand as price-led cleaning.

Xoot should feel like premium home-care infrastructure: a trust-first operating layer for households, Airbnb hosts, landlords, offices, Xooters, and internal teams.

## Customer Types

- Busy households and professionals who want their home handled without repeated planning.
- Families that need trusted recurring support, household memory, pet notes, and continuity.
- Care+ members who value priority support, saved preferences, favourite Xooters, and boosted loyalty.
- Airbnb hosts, landlords, and property managers who need turnover reliability, access notes, proof, invoices, and multi-property history.
- Small offices that need predictable recurring cleaning, room priorities, after-hours access rules, and operational records.
- Xooters who want verified profile value, trusted client flow, fair routing, performance visibility, and professional growth.
- Internal operations, support, trust, finance, and marketing teams that need role-scoped dashboards and auditability.

## Contractor Model

Xooters are independent contractors, not Xoot employees. Xoot manages discovery, booking, verification workflows, in-app communication, support routing, quality signals, and platform trust. Xoot must avoid copy or product behavior that implies employment control beyond platform standards, safety rules, verification, and booking fulfillment requirements.

Core contractor principles:

- Xooters must complete verification before going live.
- Contractors only receive client household information needed for assigned active bookings.
- Sensitive access notes are shown only inside authenticated app surfaces and only inside approved access windows.
- Contractor reliability, milestones, and badges are earned through real performance.
- Off-platform payment or booking arrangements are prohibited and must be reviewed when detected.

## Revenue Model

- One-time booking revenue through service fees and service margins.
- Recurring booking revenue through repeat household, office, Airbnb, and landlord schedules.
- Care+ membership at R99/month as a household memory, priority, loyalty, and support layer.
- B2B property and office accounts with monthly invoice workflows.
- XootCred as closed-loop loyalty credit, not cash withdrawal.
- Referral credit issued only after successful qualifying actions.
- Optional booking cover referral revenue only where a valid insurance partner and approved policy wording exist.

## Services

- Regular home cleaning.
- Recurring household cleaning.
- Express cleaning.
- Moving cleaning.
- Airbnb turnover cleaning.
- Landlord move-in, move-out, and inspection-readiness cleaning.
- Small office cleaning.
- Laundry and ironing.
- Add-ons such as ironing, windows, appliance focus, balcony, and room priorities.
- Gift cleans.
- Care+ member support, Home Memory, XootCred boost, favourite Xooter, and smart reminders.

## Operating Principles

1. Trust density: make verification, reliability, support, and accountability visible.
2. Household memory: save home preferences so repeat bookings require less effort.
3. Contractor quality consistency: use verification, reliability tracking, training, and support review to improve the 100th booking.
4. Recurring infrastructure: recurring bookings, favourite Xooters, and property profiles should feel easier than starting over.
5. Concierge support speed: problems should trigger clear routing, human review where needed, and calm customer communication.
6. Backend authority: pricing, booking state, access windows, payments, credits, referrals, roles, and support outcomes must be enforced server-side.
7. Premium restraint: avoid gimmicks, fake urgency, unsupported guarantees, fake scarcity, or discount-led language.
8. In-app first: operational communication starts in the app, with email for records and urgent channels only for time-sensitive events.
9. Data minimisation: collect only what is needed, explain why it is needed, and restrict sensitive access by role and time window.
10. Route density: scheduling and replacement logic should improve reliability, travel efficiency, contractor earnings, and customer outcomes.
