# Communications Incident Template

Reviewed: 2026-07-02

Use this template for user-facing incident communications. Replace bracketed fields and remove internal notes before sending.

## Short Status Update

Subject: Update on [affected Xoot feature]

We are currently investigating an issue affecting [feature or journey]. Your [booking/payment/account/home access] data remains [known safe status]. We have paused [affected action] while we verify the issue.

What you can do now: [safe next step].

We will update you again by [time or condition].

## Resolved Update

Subject: [Affected feature] is back to normal

The issue affecting [feature] has been resolved. We found that [plain-language cause without exposing sensitive internals].

What we did:

- [containment action]
- [verification action]
- [user-impact action]

If you were affected, [specific support path or compensation/review path].

## Rules

- Do not disclose secrets, internal exploit details, provider private identifiers, gate codes, alarm codes, private addresses, ID documents, or other users' data.
- Do not promise refunds, credits, provider fixes, insurance, or legal outcomes unless approved.
- Escalate safety, payment, legal, and account-risk communications to a human.
