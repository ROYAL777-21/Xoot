# Facebook Page — month one, four posts

**The Page is where selling is expected and appropriate.** The Groups are the actual channel and live
in `facebook-groups-replies.md`; do not confuse the two, and never comment in a group as the Page.

Company register: **warm, calm, practical.** Facebook in South Africa skews older and broader than
Instagram, and the reader here is more likely to be the person who actually books a clean than
anywhere else in this plan.

⛔ **All four wait for gate 2.** A Page whose "Visit website" button lands on a GoDaddy placeholder
is worse than no Page.

⚠️ **Set the service area to one suburb and mean it.** A Page claiming national coverage that cannot
serve a request from Durban produces exactly the bad word of mouth that costs 3× to recover from.
Widen it when suburb two opens, not before.

⛔ **Do not enable Messenger auto-replies promising a response time.** No volume exists to support
one, and an unmet auto-promise is a CPA s41 problem authored by a settings toggle.

## FB-02 — Week 2 · why home size instead of hours

> **Most booking forms ask how many hours you need. We think that is the wrong question to ask you.**
>
> You know your home. You do not know how many hours your home takes — and honestly, you should not
> have to. That is our job to know.
>
> So we ask for small, medium or large, and we work out the rest.
>
> It sounds like a small interface decision. It is actually the difference between a form you can
> fill in confidently and one where you guess and hope.

⛔ No competitor named or implied. `BRAND_GUIDE`.
`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`

## FB-03 — Week 3 · the question worth asking any service

> **A question worth asking any home service before you book — including us.**
>
> *"What happens if the person who was booked can't make it?"*
>
> It is the question almost nobody asks until the morning it happens, and it separates a service from
> a phone number. Every service should have a clear answer. Ask for it.
>
> Ours is built into the app rather than into a phone call, and you can see it before you book.

⚠️ **Only publishable once the replacement flow is genuinely reachable by a customer.** If it is
gated or unfinished at the time of posting, this becomes a capability claim about something that does
not work — CPA s41. Verify before it goes out.
`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`

## FB-04 — Week 4 · the honest one

> **We are new, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.**
>
> Xoot has been built over the last several months and is opening in one suburb first. We do not have
> years of reviews, because we do not have years.
>
> What we do have is a product you can look at before you commit to anything, and a policy of not
> claiming things we cannot show you. If you book early you will be one of the first, with everything
> good and bad that implies.
>
> If that suits you, we would like to earn it.

⚠️ **The strongest post in the set and the most likely to be softened in review.** Its whole value is
that it does not oversell — the moment "we would rather say so" becomes "we are proud to announce",
it is the same post as everyone else's and worth nothing.

⛔ Contains no price, no rating, no testimonial, no "limited slots", no launch-offer framing. **A
launch discount would breach `BRAND_GUIDE`'s ban on discount language** and the premium positioning
in `locked-commercial-facts.md` §Brand — it is not available as an option here even if it would work.
`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`
