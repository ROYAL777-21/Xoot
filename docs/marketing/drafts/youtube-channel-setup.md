# YouTube — claim it, set it up, post nothing

**Reserved and dormant, deliberately.** This file exists so the channel is *ready* rather than
*empty*, and so nobody mistakes "we have a YouTube" for "we are on YouTube".

---

## Why nothing publishes here this month

Long-form is the highest-cost format available and **Xoot has no proof to fill it with.** A
twelve-minute video needs either a story with an outcome or a body of real customer work, and zero
bookings have completed.

⚠️ **The measured argument for waiting rather than for skipping**, because the case for eventually
being here is strong:

- Hormozi's long-form beats his shorts **3.2× on median outlier**, while he ships roughly four shorts
  per long-form. Long-form is worth it — *eventually*.
- A client who switched from entertainment to educational went from 4–5K leads per video to **over
  20,000**. Educational is the register Xoot would use.

Both facts argue for YouTube **after gate 6**, when there are real bookings to teach from. Until
then Shorts are the TikTok library re-uploaded, which costs minutes — **but only once a library
exists**, and it does not yet.

## When this channel wakes up

Two conditions, both, not either:

1. **Gate 6** — 10–20 friendly bookings completed, so there is real material.
2. **A TikTok library of at least 12 published pieces**, so Shorts is a re-upload rather than a
   production line.

The first upload is then Shorts, re-cut from TikTok, at zero marginal cost. Long-form comes after
that or not at all.

⚠️ **The trap to avoid is the enthusiastic first video.** A channel with one polished intro video
posted eight months ago is a worse signal than an empty one — an empty channel reads as "not started
yet", a stale one reads as "tried and stopped."
