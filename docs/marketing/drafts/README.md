## How to review this

Every draft ends with `APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`. Reply with the draft ID and one of those three.

- **APPROVE** — the text is publishable exactly as written. Not "roughly right".
- **EDIT** — say what is wrong. A rewrite is cheap; a wrong post under your name is not.
- **KILL** — it goes. A killed draft is a useful signal, not a waste; three kills in one hook family
  tells us more than one approval does.

**`VOICE: UNCALIBRATED`** still applies to every founder draft. No writing samples have been
supplied, so these are built against *structure* — brevity, specificity, one idea, payload in line
one — and not against how you actually sound. **Correct them rather than approving them.** A wrong
voice on a personal account is worse than silence, because it is your name on it.

### ⚠️ The one that will feel most restrictive

**No prices.** It removes the easiest hook in home services, and it is not negotiable while the
conflict is open. Every draft here is written to be *better without a number* rather than to be a
version of a price post with the price removed — which is why most of them lead with a mechanism, a
defect or a decision instead.

Resolving the conflict is a decision-log entry plus a server change. Until then, a post that says
what a clean costs risks stating a figure the checkout will not honour, and that is a CPA problem
rather than an untidiness.
