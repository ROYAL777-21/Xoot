# Instagram — month one

**Secondary. Repurposed only.** 8.6M South African users 18+ against TikTok's 29.1M — this is not a
close call, and an earlier version of the plan led with Instagram by habit rather than by measurement.

## The rule that keeps this secondary

**Reels are the TikTok cuts, reframed. Nothing is shot for Instagram.**

The four Reels this month are TT-01, TT-02, TT-03 and TT-04 exported at 9:16 with the safe-area
adjusted for Instagram's UI overlay. That is the whole production cost — minutes, not a shoot.

⚠️ **The moment Instagram needs its own shoot, it has stopped being secondary** and is quietly
consuming the capacity that TikTok needs. If that starts happening, the correct response is to cut
Instagram, not to add capacity.

⚠️ **Ignore four-digit outlier scores on this platform.** Eden's Instagram index returned outliers of
2,267×, 3,109× and 585× on the reference accounts. Those are static posts on Reels-baselined
accounts: the multiplier is measuring a missing denominator, not performance. Recorded in
`../growth-log.md` and repeated here because it will look like a discovery every time someone sees
it fresh.

## The four statics

Design-kit graphics. No AI generation required, no marker needed, no consent form. Built from
`src/lib/design-tokens.ts` and the kit's shape rules so they are unmistakably the same brand as the
site.

### IG-01 — The promise
**Visual:** the locked brand promise, set in the kit's display face, on the deep-green ground.
**Text in frame:** `Your home. Handled.`
**Caption:** `Three words we have to keep. Everything we build gets measured against them.`
⛔ Nothing else in frame. No price, no service list, no app-store badge until the app exists.
`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`

### IG-02 — What a Xooter is
**Visual:** a single line of type, kit palette.
**Text in frame:** `The people who do the work are called Xooters.`
**Caption:** `Not "cleaners", not "providers". They are the reason the thing works, and the name says
so.`
⛔ **No income claim, no "join us", no recruitment framing.** LRA s200A is unsettled and this is the
post most likely to drift into recruitment by accident.
`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`

### IG-03 — The three steps
**Visual:** three stacked cards — `Pick your home size` / `Pick your day` / `Done`.
**Caption:** `That is the entire booking. We have resisted adding a fourth step twice.`
**Note:** the static version of TT-07 and brief B1. Same three cards, so the Reel and the static
reinforce rather than compete.
`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`

### IG-04 — Why home size, not hours
**Visual:** two lines, the first struck through.
**Text in frame:** ~~`How many hours do you need?`~~ → `How big is your home?`
**Caption:** `You know your home. You do not know how many hours it takes — that is our job to know.`
⛔ No competitor named or implied. "Most apps ask the first one" is a category statement and stays one.
`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`

## Cadence and metric

| | |
|---|---|
| **Reels** | 4/month — the TikTok cuts, reframed |
| **Statics** | 4/month — one a week, the set above |
| **Stories** | Not this month. A Story needs an audience to be worth the minutes |
| **Metric** | Profile visits per 1,000 impressions at 72h. **Not likes, not reach** |

⛔ **No paid promotion until gate 2 clears.** Every tap goes to a bio link that currently resolves to
a GoDaddy placeholder. Paying to send people there is the three-times-reach mistake, bought
deliberately.

⛔ **Never on this account:** a before/after presented as a real job, a customer photo, a Xooter's
face, a price, a countdown or limited-slots framing, or a generated image without its burned-in
marker.
