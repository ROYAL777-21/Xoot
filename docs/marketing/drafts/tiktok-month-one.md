## The four that can be shot today, with a phone, for nothing

**TT-01 to TT-04 are screen recordings of the real booking flow.** They lead the month for four
reasons, and I want them stated because it is tempting to start with the AI:

1. **They need no Higgsfield plan, no credits, no preset, no consent form and no AI marker.**
2. **They cannot be accused of faking anything.** The product on screen is the product.
3. They show a working thing at a moment when the company's biggest credibility problem is that
   nothing is publicly reachable.
4. Screen recordings historically outperform produced creative for software, and cost about 1% as
   much.

⚠️ **Shoot them against staging or a local build, not production**, and never with real customer
data on screen. Use a test account.

⚠️ **No prices visible in any frame.** `locked-commercial-facts.md` §Current Pricing Conflict is
open. Blur, crop, or use a test account whose totals are not shown. If the price is unavoidable in
the flow, **the shot does not get used.**

### TT-01 — "The whole booking, unedited" · 9:16 · ~25s

**Hook (0–2s), on-screen text over the first frame:**
> *Booking a home clean, start to finish. No cuts.*

**Body:** one continuous screen recording. Pick home size → pick a date → confirm. Thumb visible.
Real load times left in, including any slow frame.

**Close (last 2s):** the confirmation state, held.

**Voiceover:** none. Ambient only, or a quiet track.
**Caption:** `No edits, no cuts. This is the whole thing.`

⚠️ **Leave the slow frame in.** The uncut claim is the entire hook, and a viewer who spots a cut has
caught you in the one thing the post asserts.

### TT-02 — "How long it takes" · 9:16 · ~15s

**Hook:** a timer overlay starting at 0:00 as the first tap lands.

**Body:** the same flow, timer running. Stop the timer on the confirmation screen.

**Close:** the timer's final value, held for a beat.

**Caption:** `Timed it.`

⚠️ **Whatever the timer says is what ships.** If it reads 41 seconds, the post says 41 seconds. Do
not re-shoot for a rounder number — a falsifiable claim only works while it is actually falsifiable,
and this is the MrBeast mechanic that transfers (*"exactly 1 like in 24 hours"*), stripped of the
money.

`APPROVE / EDIT / KILL`

## The test grid — what each week is actually measuring

Format and topic are varied independently, because a winning format transfers across topics and a
winning topic does not transfer across formats. Change both at once and a good result tells you
nothing.

| Week | Posts | Variable under test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | TT-01, TT-02 | **Does uncut beat timed?** Same content, different framing device |
| 2 | TT-03, TT-04 | **Does the customer side or the Xooter side pull more?** |
| 3 | TT-05, TT-07 | **Does illustrated brand film beat explanatory?** First AI assets |
| 4 | TT-09, TT-12 | **Does a founder on camera beat a montage?** |

**Metric: profile visits per 1,000 impressions at 72 hours. Not views.**

⚠️ **The View Trap, measured on five accounts, five times out of five:** the highest-reach post had
the worst engagement rate. A 40,000-view post that sends nobody to the profile has taught you
nothing except that a hook travels.

⚠️ **No thresholds, and that is deliberate.** There is no baseline. A threshold invented before the
first post is a number graded against itself. Rank the four posts in each pair against each other,
and after week 4 there is a real baseline to set thresholds from.
