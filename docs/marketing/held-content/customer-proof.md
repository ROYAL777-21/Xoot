## Why this is written now

The day the first clean completes there will be a strong temptation to post something immediately
and get the consent later. This file exists so the sequence is already decided: **the consent form
is written before the photograph is taken**, not after.

## The consent, in plain language

Ask for it **before** the clean, at booking, not after when the customer is pleased and easy to say
yes. The specific uses are named — POPIA requires "specific", and a blanket permission is not.

> We would like to show what this clean looked like. If you are happy for us to, we will use the
> photographs on our website and social media, and we will not use your name, your address or
> anything that identifies where you live unless you tell us we can.
>
> You can change your mind at any time and we will take it down — you do not have to give a reason.
> This is not a condition of the booking and saying no changes nothing about the service.

⚠️ **"Not a condition of the booking" is load-bearing.** Consent that is a condition of service is
not voluntary, and therefore not consent.

## Before-and-after, when there is one to shoot

- Same framing, same lens, same time of day. A wider lens on the "after" is the oldest trick in
  home services and a reader who spots it disbelieves the whole account.
- **No staging of the "before".** If the room was not that bad, the photograph is a fabrication.
- The Xooter's face only with their own separate consent — they are a person, not a prop, and their
  POPIA rights are identical.

## The review script

Not a script for the customer to read. A set of questions whose honest answers make a review:

1. What was the thing you had been putting off?
2. What did you expect to go wrong that didn't?
3. What would you tell someone who was nervous about a stranger in their house?

⚠️ Question 3 is the one that matters. `hormozi-corpus-findings.md` records that **Perceived
Likelihood** — not Dream Outcome — is the binding term in this market: the customer is risk-sensitive
before she is price-sensitive. A review that answers the fear outperforms one that praises the shine.

⛔ Never offer anything for a review. A paid or incentivised review is a misleading representation,
and platform terms ban it independently.
