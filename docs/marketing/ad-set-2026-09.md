# The ad set — UGC, comparison, problem→solution

Written 2026-09-04. Built against `xoot-hooks`, `xoot-demand`, `xoot-offer-design`,
`creative-director.md`, `ad-matrix-and-avatars.md` and `BRAND_GUIDE` — **no new avatars, hooks or
brand lines are invented here.**

## 🥇 The evidence this set is built on

**Eden, measured 2026-09-04:**

1. **Dignity-of-labour is the strongest angle in this space by a wide margin.** The top post
   measured: **206.95× outlier, 14.78% engagement, 10,151 shares** — *"the person cleaning the
   restrooms in the office you work at deserves a livable wage."* ⚠️ Nothing in "satisfying cleaning
   transformation" came close. **Xoot has the substance behind that sentiment** — the R47/h floor
   and suburb matching — and `company-social-playbook.md` already calls travel-eating-pay the
   defensible, non-comparative pillar.
2. **Why UGC ads fail**, from a practitioner's own post-mortem (183K views, 1,172 comments):
   *"the hook was weak — i opened by talking about myself instead of speaking to my audience's
   actual problem. **nobody cares what i think of the product before they trust me.**"*
   → **Open on their problem. Benefits not features. Trust before opinion.**
3. **AI-slop fatigue is measurable** — the highest outlier in the whole UGC search (**615×**) was an
   essay about how exhausting inauthentic AI content has become. ⚠️ **Direct support for the
   founder's "natural, not too high energy" brief.**

**And the binding constraint, from `xoot-offer-design`:** *"In this market the binding term is
**Perceived Likelihood**… A woman booking a stranger into her home is risk-sensitive before she is
price-sensitive."* ⛔ **So every ad here moves the trust variable, not the price variable.**

# ⛔ Three things the founder asked for that are not in here, and what replaced them

| Asked | Why not | What ships instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Social proof / testimonials / reviews** | **Zero completed bookings.** CPA s41 — misleading regardless of intent | 🥇 **Proof of mechanism.** Show the thing that earns trust instead of asserting that someone trusted it |
| **Competitor comparison** | `BRAND_GUIDE` bans public competitor mentions; naming one is a defamation risk in a small market | 🥇 **Comparison against the unnamed category** — quote forms, callbacks, price-at-the-end. Sharper anyway: it is what the viewer actually experienced |
| **Before/after of a real clean** | Needs a real completed booking **and** written POPIA consent. Gate 6 | Illustrated, marked as illustration — shows what the service **is**, never what it **did** |

⚠️ **All three become available the day gate 6 clears.** `customer-ugc-pipeline.md` is built,
dormant, and blocked *only* on there being a customer.

## AD-01 · Problem → solution · avatar A × H1 · 9:16

**Form:** the screen recording, with the hook as text over the first 2 seconds.

> **0:00** *(text, over the real app)* You've walked past that same pile for eleven days.
> **0:02** It's not laziness. It's Tuesday, again.
> **0:04** *(scroll begins — the real booking surface)* Pick the service. Pick the day.
> **0:08** From R250. The price is on screen before you book.

**Objection answered:** *"I should be doing this myself"* — with permission, never shame.
**Register:** relief. **Proof:** the product itself.
**Prediction:** clears the **75–80% 3-second retention** gate — the hook is a specific observation,
not a claim. **Falsifier:** if retention is under 75%, the hook is broken; fix the hook, not the cut.

## AD-02 · Comparison, naming nobody · avatar D × H3 · 9:16

> **0:00** The way this usually goes:
> **0:02** You fill in a form. Someone phones back. Someone comes to look.
> **0:05** And you find out what it costs at the end.
> **0:07** *(cut to the real app)* Or: R250, four hours, on screen, before you book.

⛔ **No competitor named, implied, or shown.** The comparison is to a *process*, and that process is
what the viewer actually experienced last time.
**Objection:** *"what does it actually cost, and what am I getting."*

## AD-03 · Proof of mechanism · avatar D × H3 · 9:16

🥇 **The strongest honest proof Xoot has**, and it is a real, working mechanism:

> **0:00** Here's what has to happen before a job can be marked done.
> **0:03** A photo before. A photo after.
> **0:06** The button stays off until both exist.

⚠️ **True in code today** — `BookingDetail.tsx:500` disables "Complete job" until both photos are
uploaded. **This is a rule the software enforces, not a promise the marketing makes.**

## AD-04 · The dignity angle · all avatars · 9:16 or static

Built on the strongest measured signal in the whole research pass.

> **0:00** A cleaner in Johannesburg can lose half a day's pay just getting to the job.
> **0:04** So we match on how far she has to travel.
> **0:07** And there's a floor under what an hour pays. It isn't a suggestion — it's in the code.

⚠️ **Every clause is verifiable**: suburb-centroid matching in `ai-match` and `request-replacement`,
`MAX_TRAVEL_KM = 25`, and `LONG_JOB_FLOOR_CENTS_PER_HOUR = R47`.
⛔ **No income claim for any Xooter** — CPA s41, no volume exists to support one.
⛔ **Not recruitment-facing.** It describes how the company works; it does not invite anyone to
apply. **That line matters: an invitation would trip LRA s200A.**

# The 10% — the experiment

## AD-07 · The illustrated presenter, at home · avatar A × H1 · 9:16

⚠️ **This is deliberately the 10%, not the 70%, and the reason is measured twice over.**
`xoot-hooks`: *"models remain weakest at a person speaking, and the uncanny result attracts hostile
comments that poison the post. **Favour text-on-screen and demonstration formats over talking
heads.**"* Eden's 615× AI-slop finding says the same thing from the audience side.

**Setting:** a real-feeling Cape Town flat — occupied, slightly cluttered, possessions in it.
⛔ **Never a showroom.**

**Script — the only words she may say:**
> *"You've walked past that same pile for eleven days. It's not laziness — it's Tuesday, again.
> Xoot's a home-services app. You pick your home size, you pick your day, and a Xooter comes.
> From R250."*

**Marker:** `AI-generated character`, bottom-left, 5% of frame height, full duration.

### The voice — a South African coloured accent, directed honestly

The founder chose an AI voice over casting a voice actor, knowing the risk. **So the guardrails are
part of the direction, not an afterthought:**

- ✅ Direct for **a specific person, not a register** — a Cape Town woman in her late twenties
  speaking normally. ⛔ **Not "doing an accent."**
- ⛔ **No exaggerated intonation. No slang inserted for flavour. No comedic cadence.** The natural,
  low-energy register the founder asked for is also what protects against caricature — they are the
  same instruction.
- ✅ SA English used naturally where it belongs: *geyser, stoep, complex, robots, load-shedding.*
- ⚠️ **Acceptance test, and it is not automatable: a South African listener reviews every take.**
  If it lands as performance rather than speech, it is cut.

⛔ **What she may never do** (`ai-presenter-brief.md`): be photoreal, be a named or implied
customer, give a rating, or describe an outcome the product cannot deliver. *"I love how fast this
books"* is fine. *"My Xooter arrived in ten minutes"* is not.

# The launch video — three audiences, one film

Replaces the single undifferentiated D1 brief.

| Beat | Audience | Register | The moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Young professional (A) | Relief, permission | Saturday morning. The flat is handled. She leaves it |
| 2 | Household manager (C) | Control, continuity | The same Xooter, who already knows the house |
| 3 | 🥇 Intergenerational | Care, dignity | An adult child books a clean **for** her mother's home, from her own phone |

⛔ **Beat 3 must not say "gift", "voucher", or "send someone a clean."**

⚠️ **The gift product cannot deliver a gift.** `gift_clean_orders`, a writer and a routed page all
exist — but **nothing sets `paid`, `sent` or `redeemed`, nothing writes `redeemed_at`, and the
recipient is never notified.** Three of the five states are unreachable. Advertising it is worse
than a testimonial, because a viewer could act on it and fail.

✅ **Booking to another address is a normal booking and works today.** The emotional payload the
founder wanted — someone older being cared for by someone younger — survives completely. Only the
word "gift" goes.

# ⛔ Never in any of these — all verified off this session

**Tipping** (`TIP_READINESS = "schema_pending"` — the card renders nothing) · **SOS**
(`SAFETY_READINESS = "schema_pending"` — there is no button) · **any call** (`twilio.masked_voice`
false; a real call has never been placed on any surface) · **recurring Care+ billing** (there is no
recurring job) · **the ZD-035 crew price** · **the MIE wording** · **a gift being received** ·
**any testimonial, rating, star, completed job, user count or revenue figure.**

⚠️ **And the frame over all of it:** production holds **0 bookings and 1 user**, Vercel auth is on,
and the domain serves GoDaddy. **The register is "here is how it works" — never "go and do this
now."** A call to action would be false today regardless of what the ad said.

# Measurement

**Rank on profile visits per 1,000 impressions at 72 hours. Never views.**

| Gate | Threshold |
|---|---|
| 3-second retention | **75–80%** — below it, the hook is broken |
| Engagement rate | >12% is a viral candidate |
| Outperformance | 3–5× account average is a boost candidate |
| The only comment that predicts installs | *"where do I get this"* |

⚠️ **Give:ask floor — at most one in five posts contains a call to book.** Reach outruns Xooter
coverage, and a booking Xoot cannot serve is worse than no booking.
