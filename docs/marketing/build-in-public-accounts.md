# Build-in-public accounts — measured

Measured 2026-09-01 against Eden's index, for the `@TheRealSkosana` founder account.
`outlier` = a multiple of that creator's **own** baseline. Comparable within a creator, never across.

---

## The starting line, now known

`@TheRealSkosana` resolved. **1 follower. 32 posts. Following 23.** Never synced, so there is no
post-level data and no baseline yet — but the account exists and the number is the number.

Bio, verbatim:

> "The dream was always to make a billion dollar mistake". / Putting the FAANG(Z) in FAANG. /
> Forced to be a Software Engineering Grad, Vibe coder at heart.

### ⚠️ The single highest-leverage hour available, and it is the bio

The 3-second credibility test asks a visitor to learn **who you are, what you do, and what you can
do for them** before they scroll on. Compare:

| Account | Bio | Passes? |
|---|---|---|
| `@levelsio` | A list of products with MRR beside each: `$80K/m`, `$23K/m`, `$20K/m`, `$20K/m` | ✅ Proof *is* the bio |
| `@jacobrodri_` | "founder [appkittie] — find winning apps, replicate what's working. tweets about growth and my learnings" | ✅ Who / what / what for you, in 14 words |
| `@TheRealSkosana` | Three jokes | ❌ Funny. Says nothing about Xoot, home services, South Africa, or what a reader gets |

The jokes are not the problem — the *absence* is. Nothing in that bio tells a visitor a company
exists, what it does, or why to follow. At 1 follower, that is the constraint before content is.

## The pattern that holds across every account measured

| Creator | Followers | Posts | **Median outlier** | Avg outlier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `@levelsio` | 945.1K | 463 (last year) | **0.82×** | 2.05× |
| `@jacobrodri_` | **36.7K** | 1,044 | **0.77×** | 5.36× |
| `@alexhormozi` (YT) | 4.45M | 2,476 | 1.97× | 4.73× |

**Every one of them has a median below their own mean, and two are below 1×.** More than half of
what these accounts publish underperforms their own baseline. A handful of posts carry everything.

Which produces the finding that matters most, now confirmed on three independent accounts:

> **They publish most of what performs worst.** Hormozi ships four shorts (1.59×) per long-form
> (5.09×). levelsio's best format is the thread at **4.09× median** — he posted **5 of them out of
> 463**, against 413 plain tweets at **0.78×**. His best topic is Startups at **5.41× median** —
> 11 posts of 463, 2.4% of his output, while Technology at 1.48× gets 127.

**Copying a big account's posting mix copies their mistakes.** Copy what performs, not what they do.

**Not one is about his own product.** Every single one is a short reaction line attached to
*somebody else's* artefact, usually with a real number in it. The shape is:

> **[one casual line of reaction] + [someone else's video] + [a number you can check]**

That is **curated discovery**, not building in public. It is a genuinely strong format and it is
reproducible at small scale — but naming him as the build-in-public reference was wrong, and the
data says so plainly. What transfers to Xoot is the *shape*: point at a real thing, react in one
line, include a number. Xoot's version points at the SA home-services market, not at apps.

Note the engagement column: his lowest-reach post has **six times the engagement rate** of his
highest. The View Trap, again, on a fourth account.

## `@levelsio` — 945.1K followers, the actual build-in-public archetype

463 posts in the last year, avg 383K views, **median outlier 0.82×**.

### His top posts are mostly not about building anything

| Outlier | Views | Eng. rate | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 49.32× | 1.89M | 2.35% | A stylised map image, no text |
| 34.98× | 8.25M | 0.43% | A bus crash video in France |
| 21.59× | 1.69M | 1.14% | A rant: "I'm so done with real estate agents" |
| 17.35× | 2.29M | 0.73% | AI video generation news |
| 15.92× | **26.6M** | **0.06%** | EU politics |
| **15.64×** | 615K | **2.36%** | **"how to build a bootstrapped startup without funding:" — a 10-point list** |

**Read the last two rows together.** The politics post got 26.6 million views at a 0.06%
engagement rate. The bootstrapping list got 615,000 views — 43× less reach — at **2.36%, the
highest engagement rate in his top eight, ~39× better.**

That is the whole argument in two rows. **The off-topic outrage buys reach; the build-in-public
teaching buys engagement.** Xoot needs the second one. Reach from a bus crash converts nobody, and
`founder-voice.md` already bars the political register for reasons that have nothing to do with
performance.

### What actually transfers

- **The numbered list of hard-won rules.** "how to build a bootstrapped startup without funding:"
  then ten numbered lines, each a complete instruction with no throat-clearing. Highest engagement
  in his set. Xoot has the raw material for this already — deploy ordering, the pricing-parity
  rule, the `_shared` bundling trap — each learned from a real failure.
- **Radical transparency as identity.** His bio *is* his revenue. The proof is not a claim.
- **Format: thread 4.09× vs tweet 0.78×.** A 5.3× difference he almost never uses. Xoot should use
  it deliberately — a build-in-public week genuinely has sequence.
- **Mood: educational avg 4.80× beats informative 1.52×.** Teach, don't announce. Same finding as
  Hormozi's educational-over-inspirational.

### What does not transfer

The EU politics, the profession-bashing ("what an absolutely useless profession"), and the
"the rest of the world can't really do startups" post — which, from a South African founder, would
be an own goal in front of the exact audience Xoot needs.

## What this changes for `@TheRealSkosana`

1. **Fix the bio first.** One hour. Who / what / what a reader gets. It gates everything.
2. **Build-in-public is the register; the numbered-rules list is the format.** levelsio's
   highest-engagement post is a list of rules learned the hard way. This repo generates those
   weekly without being asked.
3. **Use threads deliberately.** 4.09× vs 0.78× on the only account measured that has both.
4. **Steal Rodri's shape, not his subject.** One casual line + a real artefact + a checkable number.
5. **Judge on engagement rate, not reach.** Four accounts now show the highest-reach post having
   the worst engagement. At 1 follower, reach is not the constraint anyway — being worth following
   is.
6. **Do not copy anyone's posting mix.** All three publish most of what performs worst.
