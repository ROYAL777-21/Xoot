# Platform playbooks — eight presences, two content engines

Written 2026-09-02. One page per platform: what it is for, what never goes on it, cadence, the
metric that judges it.

**Substack added 2026-09-02 at the founder's request**, and it changes the shape of the argument
below rather than just extending the list — see its section. It is the only channel here that is
not rented.

---

## ⚠️ Why eight platforms are claimed and only two are published on

The founder asked for content across seven, then added Substack. Claiming eight is right.
**Publishing on eight is the documented way this fails**, and the file that says so is already in
this repo:

> *"Company platforms go sequential, not parallel. A solo creator runs one primary + one secondary,
> with an 'Eye of Sauron' rotation innovating on one at a time. The four-platform plan is what he
> would call diluted — and 73% of people who start making content quit inside a year."*
> — `growth-log.md`, 2026-09-02, from the Ralston transcript

That was written about **four** platforms. Eight is not an improvement on four.

**So: claim all eight handles this week — cheap, correct, stops squatting. Produce for two.** The
rest carry repurposed work or a single artefact, at a cost measured in minutes.

The two engines are **TikTok** (company) and **X** (founder). Everything else in this table is a
resize, a repost, a conversation, or a placeholder — and that is the point.

| Platform | Role | Weekly cost |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | 🟢 Primary engine | Full production |
| **Instagram** | 🟢 Secondary — Reels repurposed | ~30 min |
| **Facebook** | 🟡 Page + **Groups** — the underrated one | Participation, not posting |
| **X** | 🟡 Founder, text | 1 post/day |
| **Substack** | 🟡 **Founder, long-form — the only owned audience** | 1 essay/week |
| **LinkedIn** | 🟡 Founder, repurposed from X | ~15 min |

## 🟢 TikTok — primary

**Why primary:** **29.1M South African users 18+**, against Instagram's 8.6M
(`company-social-playbook.md`). It is not close, and the plan previously led with Instagram.

**What goes here:** the six-cell test grid from `ad-matrix-and-avatars.md` — AI presenter pieces,
text-on-still, and above all **screen recordings of the real booking flow**, which cost nothing and
cannot be faked.

**Never:** competitor comparisons (`BRAND_GUIDE`), price-led hooks, any capability behind a
readiness constant, fake urgency.

**Metric:** profile visits per 1,000 impressions at 72h. **Not views.** On five accounts measured,
the highest-reach post had the worst engagement rate every time.

**Cadence:** set against capacity in six months, not this week.

## 🟢 Instagram — secondary, repurposed only

Reels are the TikTok cut, reframed. Static posts carry the text-on-still cells.

⚠️ **Do not treat Instagram as a second production line.** It is the same work, resized. The moment
it needs its own shoot, it has stopped being secondary.

⚠️ **Ignore four-digit outlier scores here.** Eden's Instagram index returned outliers of 2,267×,
3,109× and 585× — those are static posts on Reels-baselined accounts, and the multiplier is
measuring a missing denominator, not performance (`growth-log.md`).

## 🟡 Facebook — and the value is not the feed

Facebook in South Africa skews older and broader than Instagram. But the reason it matters here is
narrower and better than reach.

### Suburb community groups are where the demand actually asks

*"Can anyone recommend someone reliable?"* appears weekly in every Fourways, Sea Point, Bryanston
and Table View community group. That is **demand with intent, in a named suburb** — precisely what
`launch-protocol.md` gate 5 is about.

### ⚠️ It is also the fastest channel to get thrown out of

Dropping promotional posts into a community group gets the account removed and generates hostility
in exactly the community Xoot needs. The rules are not optional:

1. **The founder participates as a person, from the personal account.** Not the Page.
2. **Answer the question that was asked.** If someone asks about load-shedding, answer about
   load-shedding.
3. **Never pitch unprompted.** When someone asks for a recommendation and Xoot genuinely fits, say
   so once, disclose the connection in the same sentence, and stop.
4. **Read each group's rules before posting.** Most ban promotion outright; some have a
   business-post day. Both are workable, and ignoring either is not.
5. **Never argue in a group.** A founder's public argument is the screenshot that outlives it.

### What the Page and Marketplace are for

The Page and a Marketplace / local-services listing are the places where selling is expected and
appropriate. Feature announcements, service area, contact.

**Metric:** enquiries by suburb. Not page likes.

## 🟡 Substack — the only channel here that is not rented

Added at the founder's request, 2026-09-02.

### Why it is worth a slot when seven were already too many

**Every other channel on this page is rented.** X, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn each
decide how many of the people who chose to follow Xoot actually see it, and each has changed that
answer without warning. An email list is the only asset here that survives an algorithm change,
because delivery does not pass through anyone's ranking model.

It is also the only place the founder's material has **natural length**. The GoDaddy discovery is a
1,400-word essay with a real mechanism, three false conclusions and a correction. Compressed to 280
characters it loses the part that makes it worth reading. That essay has nowhere else to go.

### ⚠️ Two constraints, both permanent, both recorded so nobody re-litigates them

**1. Substack has no public posting API.** Eden indexes Substack for *analysis* — it can measure a
publication, and that is genuinely useful — but nothing in this stack can publish to it. **Automation
stops at the draft, permanently.** Every essay is a manual paste, and no roadmap changes that.

**2. At zero subscribers an essay is a broadcast to nobody.** This is the honest near-term case: its
value is **archive and credibility link**, not reach. Someone who sees a good X post and wants more
needs somewhere to go, and a Substack archive is a better answer than a profile with 32 posts. Reach
comes later or not at all, and the plan does not depend on it.

Both of those argue for the same cadence: **one essay a week, and no more.** Weekly is sustainable
against a founder's actual week and produces an archive worth linking to inside a month.

### What goes here

Long-form build-in-public. The register is the founder's — *futuristic and sharp*, per
`BRAND_GUIDE`'s two-register rule — and the subject is always **a thing that actually happened, with
the number attached**. The four drafted essays in `drafts/substack-month-one.md` are each built on a
defect in this repo that has a measurement behind it.

**Substack Notes is X-shaped.** The week's X drafts repurpose there at close to zero marginal cost,
which is the main reason Substack does not become a third production line.

### Never

- ⛔ A customer story. Zero bookings exist, and a long-form format makes a fabricated one *more*
  convincing, not less — which under **CPA s41** makes it worse, not better.
- ⛔ A price *in isolation*. The conflict that made this an absolute ban closed with ZD-037 on
  2026-09-03 — the locked document and `basePrice()` now agree — so an essay **may** state the
  ladder, provided it states the hours with it. What stays banned is a bare rand figure with no
  scope attached: a reader holds Xoot to "R250 a clean", and the four hours are what make it true.
- ⛔ A paid tier. Charging for a newsletter before the actual product takes money is the wrong first
  transaction.
- ⛔ Cross-posting an essay verbatim to LinkedIn. Same idea, different room — the LinkedIn drafts are
  written separately for exactly this reason.

**Metric:** subscribers per 1,000 X impressions. That measures whether the founder account is
converting attention into something owned, which is the only reason this channel exists.

⛔ **Fix the X bio before the first essay ships.** The bio is the path to Substack; without it the
essay is unreachable and the metric above cannot move.

## ⚪ YouTube — reserved and dormant, deliberately

**Claim the handle. Post nothing yet.**

Shorts are the TikTok library re-uploaded, which costs minutes — but only once a library exists.
Long-form is the highest-cost format available and Xoot has no proof to fill it with.

⚠️ **The measured argument for waiting:** Hormozi's long-form beats his shorts 3.2× on median
outlier while he ships four shorts per long-form — so long-form is worth it *eventually*. And a
client who switched from entertainment to educational went from 4–5K leads per video to **over
20,000**. Both facts argue for YouTube **after** gate 6, when there are real bookings to teach from.

One artefact: the Xooter ad, already written in `xooter-hiring-ad.md` with an Indeed-shaped version.

⛔ **Blocked on one decision, not on writing.** Employment classification is undefined anywhere in

confirmed by an attorney before it posts.

never recruited.

## The rules that apply everywhere

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| No claim about a capability behind a readiness constant | `TIP_READINESS`, `SAFETY_READINESS`, `canOfferTip`, `canArmSos` |
| No price that contradicts the locked facts | `locked-commercial-facts.md`, changed only by decision-log entry |
| Never `cheap` / `budget` / `discount` / `bargain` | `BRAND_GUIDE` §Copy Tone |
| No fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake reviews, invented credentials | `BRAND_GUIDE` |
| No public competitor mentions | `BRAND_GUIDE` |
| No income claim for Xooters | CPA s41 — no volume exists to support one |
| Every AI-generated asset carries its marker | Meta and TikTok both require disclosure |
| Two registers, never blurred | Founder is futuristic and sharp; company is warm, calm, practical |
| **Nothing publishes without the founder approving that exact text** | `founder-voice.md` |
