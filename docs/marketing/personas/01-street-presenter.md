## Hypothesis

**Avatar A does not believe brands, and does believe people who look like their friends.**

The objection that stops Avatar A booking is *"I can do it myself, I just don't"* — guilt, not
money. A polished brand voice makes that guilt worse and gets scrolled. A composed, unglamorous
person standing on an ordinary street, who names the feeling without selling at it, should out-hold
a produced brand asset on the same hook.

The visual bet underneath it: **every cleaning brand shoots an empty white kitchen.** This is the
opposite, and being visually unlike the category is worth more than being polished within it.

## Retirement condition — written before it runs

Retired if **any** of these hold after twelve assets, or eight weeks, whichever is later:

1. The no-presenter control (cell 2) beats her on profile visits per 1,000 impressions **twice** on
   the same hook family. One loss is noise; two on the same hook is an answer.
2. She cannot be reproduced consistently — if more than one asset in six needs regenerating for
   drift, the Soul is not doing its job and the consistency premium is unpaid.
3. A garment-text or anatomy artefact reaches a published asset. One is a mistake; a second means
   the pipeline is not safe at volume.
4. A third persona is proposed and she is the weaker of the two live. Rule 2 of the registry.

⚠️ **Eight weeks, not two.** The corpus warning applies directly: a cheaply-acquired result can be
the worst one you have, and you cannot tell on a fortnight of data.
