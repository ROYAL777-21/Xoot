# UGC and ad production briefs

Written 2026-09-02. Turns the six-cell grid in `ad-matrix-and-avatars.md` into briefs someone can
shoot. Scripts, aspect ratios, on-screen text, and the disclosure requirement.

---

## ⚠️ UGC without users is not UGC

The founder asked for UGC. The honest position: **Xoot has zero completed bookings**, so there are
no users to generate content. Anything presented as a customer's own experience today would be
fabricated — CPA s41, the ARB's testimonial rules, and `BRAND_GUIDE`'s ban on fake reviews all point
the same way.

**So there are three tiers, and only two are available now:**

| Tier | What it is | Available |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder-led** | The founder on camera, as himself, saying true things | ✅ Now |
| **Presenter-led** | The AI presenter, clearly marked, never claiming to be a customer | ✅ Once the Higgsfield plan allows |
| **Customer UGC** | A real customer, with written consent | ⛔ Gate 6 |

**The third tier is the one that actually converts**, and it cannot be manufactured earlier. That is
the whole argument for gate 6 existing.

## Cell 2 — the control, and the only one producible today

**Avatar A · Hook H1 (guilt-name) · Text-on-still · No presenter**

This is the baseline everything else is measured against. If it beats cells 1 and 3, the AI
presenter is not earning its credits.

- **Format:** static, 1080×1350 (IG) and 1080×1920 (TikTok)
- **On-screen text:** *"You've walked past that same pile for eleven days. It's not laziness. It's Tuesday, again."*
- **Design:** design-kit palette, one line, high contrast, no product shot, no logo above the fold
- **Caption:** one sentence naming the service and the suburbs. No price. No CTA beyond the profile link
- **Cost:** zero

⚠️ **Ship this even if the presenter never gets made.** A control with nothing to compare against is
still the cheapest read on whether the hook works at all.

## Cells 4–6 — screen recordings, and the best assets available

**All blocked on a reachable site.** They are also the cheapest and most credible things in this
document: a real product, recorded, with nothing to fake.

| Cell | What to record | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Booking flow end to end: address → match → confirm | H4 — "Book Nomsa again. She already knows about the cat." |
| 5 | The admin vetting fields, as a person reviews an application | H3 — "Here's what we check before someone gets your address." |
| 6 | Same as 4, framed for an Airbnb turnover | H4 |

**Cell 4 first.** Ralston's "proof over promise" and Hormozi's version of the same idea both land
here: a real screen recording out-argues produced creative, and costs a screen recorder.

⚠️ **Cell 5's claim is the reworded one from `NEEDS-FOUNDER.md` §4.** *"No Xooter is activated until
an admin has reviewed their ID, selfie, proof of address, banking details and references."* **Do not
restore the MIE wording** — the field exists, the bureau contract does not.

## Founder-led, and available today

The one tier needing no plan, no site and no customers. The founder, on camera or in text, saying
things that are true.

- **X and LinkedIn:** the seven drafts in `x-drafts-week-one.md`
- **TikTok/Reels, 30–60s:** the founder telling the GoDaddy story — three weeks, two correct builds,
  a placeholder, and the one-line comment that stopped anyone looking. It is a good story, it is
  true, and it is about competence rather than cleaning

⚠️ **Founder register, not company register.** Never reuse a founder cut as company copy — a founder
building in public and a service brand reassuring someone about a stranger in their home should not
sound alike.
