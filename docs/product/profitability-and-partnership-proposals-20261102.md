## Recommended profitability pilot

Start with a recurring office/Airbnb service agreement with minimum monthly visits and grouped
geographic/time windows. Sell reliable planned capacity rather than an unpriced discount.
The likely gain is fewer acquisition/admin interactions and less unproductive travel per paid
visit. It depends on real demand, verified available Xooters and an account-level contribution
calculation; there is no measured profit uplift to claim today.

For each proposed account, quote against worker compensation + gateway/payout costs + transport/
supplies + support/insurance/refund allowance + rewards liability + applicable payroll/tax costs
+ a positive contribution margin. Do not price an employed service using contractor commission
alone. Propose a small pilot, measure completed visits, travel/support time, cancellations,
retention and contribution, then agree a price/commitment before implementing any new plan.

Two additional proposals need explicit benefit decisions:

- Define each12/36milestone reward as a capped four-hour Regular service value ofR250, with
  upgrades/extras paid separately. The R39 booking fee is still charged unless a genuine Care+
  membership independently waives it. Worker pay stays funded. This cap remains UNAPPROVED;
  the already approved12then24more milestone sequence is unchanged.
- Review Care+ economics before adding another automatic discount. At the modelled20% commission
  floor, a four-hour member visit contributesR14.42 after assumed card fees, versusR76.06 for a
  nonmember. A paidR99cycle contributes aboutR93.06 before other benefits/overheads. Those are
  contribution estimates, not net profit. Compare retention and member frequency before pitching
  a revised benefit for new memberships; honour existing paid terms. No benefit change is made.

The [executed reward model](../release/rewards-economics-20261101.md) records assumptions and
exclusions. Actual PayFast contracted fees, VAT treatment, refund/support costs and provider
payout terms still need evidence. Do not fund growth by reducing already promised Xooter pay
or claiming an unlimited gift/reward balance that has not been settled.

## Partner wants Xooters as employees

There are three materially different operating models. The real supervision, pay and working
relationship determines the obligations; a label or a separate company does not decide them.

| Model | Who employs/pays the people? | What Xoot sells |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner employs directly | Partner signs employment contracts and runs payroll/leave/safety and applicable statutory registrations | Recruitment/placement or ongoing software/scheduling/support, under a separate agreed fee |
| Xoot managed cleaning service | Xoot is a genuine employer, manages its workforce and delivers an agreed cleaning outcome | A service agreement/SLA whose price funds loaded employment cost and margin |
| Labour supply / temporary employment service | Xoot supplies paid workers who work under the client's operational control | A staffing service, subject to LRA/TES rules and applicable registration/compliance review |

For qualifying below-threshold workers supplied beyond three months who are not performing a
statutory temporary service, LRA198A can deem the client the employer for LRA purposes. The
Constitutional Court upheld the sole-employer interpretation in
[Assign Services](https://www.concourt.org.za/index.php/judgement/252-assign-services-pty-limited-v-national-union-of-metalworkers-union-of-south-africa-and-others-casual-workers-advice-office-as-amicus-curiae).
This is not a universal three-month exemption or a way to avoid obligations by rotating staff.
The [CCMA-hosted LRA](https://smelaboursupport.ccma.org.za/images/pdf/Labour-Relations-Act-No-66-of-1995.pdf)
also contains employee-presumption and shared-liability provisions. A genuinely managed service
must be assessed on its facts rather than assumed outside TES rules.

Before offering an employed pilot: agree the real employer, duties/hours/location, supervision,
wages/leave/overtime, equipment/transport, health/safety, insurance, liability/disputes, data roles,
termination and what happens to existing Xooter contractor bookings. Give each worker a genuine
employment offer/choice; do not flip the platform's contractor role into employee status silently.
Obtain South African employment/payroll advice on the exact service/sector and registrations.

Payroll must account for applicable PAYE, UIF, SDL and COIDA obligations, not marketplace wallet
settlement. See [SARS employer guidance](https://www.sars.gov.za/guide-for-employers-in-respect-of-employees-tax-2027/).
Use the applicable sectoral/bargaining-council rates and rules. The Department's
[2026 wage notice](https://www.labour.gov.za/Media-Desk/Media-Statements/Pages/Minister-of-Employment-and-Labour%2C-Meth-increases-the-statutory-National-Minimum-Wage-to-R30%2C23-per-hour.aspx)
also points to contract-cleaning sector rates; do not assume the general minimum is the full
loaded cost or the correct rate for every cleaning contract.

Recommendation for discussion: a partner-direct-employment pilot if the partner wants its own
permanent staff, or a genuinely managed Xoot service if it wants cleaning outcomes. Choose after
the partner's actual requirements and a costed legal/payroll review; no entity or contract is
created and no worker employment decision is made here.
