# Concierge Quality Evaluation

Track:

- resolution rate;
- fallback rate;
- handoff rate;
- repeat-contact rate;
- unsafe request rate;
- unsupported-answer rate;
- hallucination or policy-conflict reports;
- wrong-intent classification;
- human override rate;
- latency and tool failure rate.

Quality reviews may propose knowledge updates, tests, copy improvements, intent improvements or tool improvements. They may not approve their own policy changes or expand Concierge authority.
