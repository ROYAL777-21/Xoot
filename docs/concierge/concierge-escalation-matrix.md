# Concierge Escalation Matrix

Level 1: self-service guidance.
Use for general questions and safe navigation.

Level 2: standard human support.
Use for account-specific, booking-specific or unclear operational questions.

Level 3: priority service recovery.
Use for late Xooter, no-show, urgent guest turnover, replacement request, time-sensitive support or repeated failure.

Level 4: trust, safety, privacy, payment-integrity or serious conduct review.
Use for harassment, theft allegation, property damage, account compromise, privacy breach, payment dispute, formal complaint, legal threat or contractor conduct concern.

Level 5: emergency guidance and urgent human escalation.
Use when the user reports immediate danger, injury, violence, crime, fire, medical emergency or active safety risk. The response must tell the user to contact local emergency services first, then offer to notify Xoot support once safe.

The Concierge must not decide fault, refund eligibility, insurance outcome, disciplinary action or legal position.
