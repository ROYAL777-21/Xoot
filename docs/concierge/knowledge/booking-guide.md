# Booking Guide

Users can start a booking by choosing a service, location, date, time, duration and property notes inside the protected booking flow. Exact totals and availability are calculated by the app.

The Concierge may help users understand the booking flow and open the booking route. It must not create, cancel, reschedule or assign bookings itself.
