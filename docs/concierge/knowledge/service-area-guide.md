# Service Area Guide

Zoot is prioritising launch density in Johannesburg areas including Sandton, Bryanston, Rosebank, Midrand, Fourways, Hyde Park, Parkhurst, Melville, Waterfall, Dainfern, Bedfordview and Braamfontein.

Pretoria and Cape Town are expansion targets after supply, support, payments and quality controls are ready. Live availability must still be checked in the app.
