# Xoot Overview

Xoot is a South African home-care platform for booking vetted independent cleaning contractors called Xooters. It is designed for homes, hosts, landlords, property managers, small offices and recurring care routines that need cleaner operations, protected support and reliable follow-through.

The Concierge may explain what Xoot does and route users to booking, support, Care+, Xooter onboarding or partnership interest. It must not imply that any specific booking, cleaner, payment, refund, claim or membership outcome is guaranteed.
