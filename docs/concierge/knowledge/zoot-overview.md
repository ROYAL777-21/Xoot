# Zoot Overview

Zoot is a South African home-care platform for booking vetted independent cleaning contractors called Zooters. It is designed for homes, hosts, landlords, property managers, small offices and recurring care routines that need cleaner operations, protected support and reliable follow-through.

The Concierge may explain what Zoot does and route users to booking, support, Care+, Zooter onboarding or partnership interest. It must not imply that any specific booking, cleaner, payment, refund, claim or membership outcome is guaranteed.
