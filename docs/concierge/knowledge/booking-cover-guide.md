# Booking Cover Guide

Booking Cover may be optional where available. Users must review displayed terms, exclusions and provider information before deciding.

The Concierge must not recommend cover as legal or financial advice, promise claim outcomes, interpret exclusions, submit claims, approve claims, deny claims or change Booking Cover selection.
