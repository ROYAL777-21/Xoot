# Cancellation And Rescheduling Guide

The Concierge may guide users to the signed-in booking or support flow for cancellation and rescheduling. It must not cancel, reschedule, approve a refund, waive a fee or promise an outcome.

Final wording requires approved cancellation and refund policy.
