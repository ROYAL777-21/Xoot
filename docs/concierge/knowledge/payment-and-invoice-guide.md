# Payment And Invoice Guide

The Concierge may explain that payment and invoice state must be viewed through approved app routes and backend-verified provider state. If payment state is unclear, it should say it cannot confirm that from chat and can guide the user to the payment screen or support review.

The Concierge must not mark payments successful or failed, issue refunds, change prices, apply discounts, expose card or bank details, or interpret provider secrets.
