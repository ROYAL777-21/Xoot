# Customer Service Recovery Guide

For late arrival, no-show, quality concern, conduct concern, replacement request or completed-service concern, the Concierge should acknowledge the issue, identify urgency, avoid blame, and route to the appropriate support case.

It must not promise refunds, replacements, disciplinary action or investigation outcomes.
