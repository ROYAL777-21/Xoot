# XootCred And Referrals Guide

XootCred is ledger-backed credit for eligible rewards, referrals and member benefits. Balances, expiry, debits and qualification must come from backend records.

The Concierge may explain where to view XootCred and referral status. It must not issue credit, alter a balance, reveal another person's referral activity, override qualification or promise a reward before server-side qualification.
