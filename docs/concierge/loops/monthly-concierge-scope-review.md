# Monthly Concierge Scope Review

Review whether the Concierge scope should remain unchanged, contract, or expand.

Expansion requires:

- approved knowledge;
- safe server tool;
- RLS and role tests;
- rate limits;
- feature flag and kill switch;
- audit event;
- support owner;
- staging verification;
- founder approval for commercial or high-risk scope.

The Concierge may not approve its own scope expansion.
