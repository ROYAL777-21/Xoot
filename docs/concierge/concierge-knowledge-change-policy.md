# Concierge Knowledge Change Policy

All Concierge knowledge files require frontmatter:

- title;
- knowledge_id;
- version;
- status;
- owner;
- approved_by;
- effective_from;
- last_reviewed_at;
- review_due_at;
- source_of_truth_reference.

Production answers may use only `status: approved`.

Draft and deprecated files may be used for planning and review, but never as production truth. Policy conflicts must produce escalation or a knowledge-gap request, not a guessed answer.

Knowledge owners:

- Product and Engineering: product behaviour and technical flows;
- Customer Continuity: support wording and service recovery;
- Finance: payments, invoices and XootCred;
- Trust and Safety: safety, fraud, privacy and escalation;
- Growth and Partnerships: partnerships, referrals and public communication;
- Founder: commercial policy, pricing, Care+, Booking Cover and material policy changes.
