# Zoot SEO Blog Plan

Reviewed: 2026-06-08

This plan turns the launch blog into a repeatable content system. Semrush MCP access is currently unavailable on the active Semrush plan, so keyword volume, difficulty, and competitor rankings still need validation once plan access is enabled.

## Publishing Cadence

- Launch phase: 1 to 2 posts per day for the first 14 days if review capacity exists.
- Steady phase: 2 to 3 high-quality posts per week.
- Google Business Profile: 3 posts per week with local service-area language.
- Every post needs human review before publishing.

## Post Formula

1. Human hook based on a real situation.
2. Clear local keyword in title and first section.
3. Explain the problem before selling the service.
4. Include Zoot's trust-first difference.
5. Mention relevant launch areas honestly.
6. Add practical checklist or decision framework.
7. End with a booking or Care+ CTA.
8. Add FAQ answers for long-tail queries.

## Quality Rules

- Do not publish fake testimonials or fake reviews.
- Do not claim guaranteed availability unless operations can fulfill it.
- Do not shame messy homes.
- Do not attack competitors.
- Do not overpromise insurance, safety, refunds, or screening beyond verified policy.
- Do not create pages for suburbs Zoot cannot reasonably serve.
- Keep contractor dignity central: Zooters are skilled professionals.
