# Brand Guide

## Brand Positioning

Zoot is premium home-care infrastructure for South African homes, hosts, offices, and Zooters. It should never feel like a cheap cleaning marketplace, a loose gig app, or a generic booking form. Every surface should communicate trust, continuity, calm support, and a home that feels handled.

Core line: `Your home. Handled.`

Care+ line: `Care+ keeps your home remembered, protected, and ready.`

## Colour Direction

- Warm base: cream, soft beige, off-white, and warm neutrals for premium calm.
- Primary warmth: terracotta or warm clay tones for action, warmth, and South African hospitality.
- Trust accent: sage and deep green for verification, support, and care states.
- Dark mode: deep ink, warm charcoal, rich navy, or dark plum surfaces with lighter panels instead of heavy shadows.
- Avoid: harsh discount red, neon palettes, childish brights, noisy gradients, and a one-note purple or beige-only interface.

## Typography

- Use a confident display face for headings, currently aligned with Space Grotesk in the app.
- Use a clean body face, currently aligned with DM Sans in the app.
- Keep hierarchy strong: page titles should feel editorial and premium; dashboard labels should stay compact and scannable.
- Do not use decorative, novelty, or overly playful type.
- Avoid negative letter spacing and viewport-scaled font sizes.

## Copy Tone

Zoot copy should be warm, calm, practical, trustworthy, and human. It should sell outcomes and relief, not labour or price.

Use:

- `trusted`, `premium`, `reliable`, `handled`, `ready`, `remembered`, `protected`, `peace of mind`
- `join Care+` or `become a Care+ member`
- `Book Nomsa again` or `Book [Zooter Name] again`
- `priority replacement support` when the backend prioritises matching

Avoid in user-facing copy:

- `cheap`, `budget`, `discount`, `bargain`
- `subscribe` for Care+
- `guaranteed replacement` unless backend, operations, and legal wording support it
- fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake reviews, invented credentials, or unsupported insurance/legal claims
- public competitor mentions

## UX Principles

- Build the real usable experience first, not a marketing-only shell.
- Make booking, rebooking, support, and property management feel direct and calm.
- Use generous spacing on public pages and denser, scannable layouts in operational dashboards.
- Use cards only for repeated items, modals, and framed tools; avoid cards nested inside cards.
- Make mobile layouts first-class: no clipped controls, no blocked scroll, no text overflow, no hidden primary actions.
- Use subtle hover, focus, loading, pressed, and haptic feedback where supported.
- Keep controls familiar: icons for tool actions, tabs for views, toggles for binary choices, sliders or inputs for numeric choices, menus for option sets.
- All sensitive actions must clearly communicate what will happen and where human review is required.

## Onboarding Principles

- Start with value before data collection.
- Frame home details as personalisation: `Help your Zooter do their best work.`
- Show pricing and expected time before account creation where feasible.
- Ask for sensitive fields only when needed, explain why, and keep them inside authenticated app surfaces.
- Do not overwhelm first-time users with every advanced feature at once.
- Returning users should see a personalised recommendation, not an empty form.

## Care+ Positioning

Care+ is not a discount plan. It is the household reliability, memory, priority, and loyalty layer.

Care+ should make R99/month feel justified through:

- Home Memory.
- Favourite Zooter continuity.
- `Book [Zooter Name] again` rebooking.
- Priority booking windows.
- Priority replacement support without unsupported guarantees.
- R30 monthly add-on credit tied to completed bookings.
- 1.5x ZootCred on completed bookings.
- Smart reminders.
- Property profiles.
- Priority support for booking issues, cancellations, and service concerns.

Care+ should feel like: `A membership for homes that should not need constant managing.`
