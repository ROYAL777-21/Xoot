# Illustration

Illustration is secondary to real service photography and functional iconography.

Allowed uses include onboarding empty states, privacy-safe education, neutral system diagrams, and rare celebratory moments. The style should be geometric, light, and warm, with simple shapes derived from approved brand tokens.

Do not use literal flowers or botanical decoration unless the owner explicitly approves it. Do not create fake logos, certification marks, partner marks, maps, reviews, people, or product screenshots. Missing assets must be created through the approved Figma or image-generation workflow and reviewed before implementation.

Illustrations need a purpose, source record, accessible alternative, dark/light variant where required, and a measured file-size budget.
