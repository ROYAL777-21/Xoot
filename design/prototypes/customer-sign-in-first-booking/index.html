Required detailed-design states

The selected direction must still be completed in Figma.

Exploration proves product character, not production completeness. The owner-approved flow must include every state below before implementation.

  • Loading, empty, partial, stale and offline
  • Invalid or out-of-area address
  • No availability and changed availability
  • Sign-in, callback, expiry and recovery errors
  • Payment pending, failed, cancelled and unknown
  • Duplicate submission and timeout recovery
  • Large text and keyboard-open layouts
  • VoiceOver, TalkBack and web focus order
  • Reduced motion and reduced transparency
  • Preferred-Zooter conflict and substitution choice

Motion and material contract

Responsive, never performative

Tap

100-180 ms tactile compression with immediate selection clarity

Step change

220-320 ms continuity without delaying focus or navigation

Verified success

350-500 ms restrained confirmation only after backend truth

Reduced motion

Render the settled state immediately and preserve all status information

Clear glass

Top-right light on web and Android fallback, with no idle shimmer or glass-on-glass

Modal privacy

A separate backdrop makes underlying text unreadable while content stays opaque
