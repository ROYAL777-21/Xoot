# Photography

Photography should reveal the actual service, person, place, or product state. It is evidence, not atmosphere.

## Subjects

- real South African homes, offices, short-stay properties, and property-management settings;
- clear before, during, or completed service moments where consent allows;
- professional Zooters shown respectfully and safely;
- visible tools, surfaces, and outcomes relevant to the service.

## Art direction

Use bright natural light, accurate colour, legible spaces, and honest scale. Avoid dark overlays, extreme crops, generic stock poses, staged luxury that misrepresents the product, and blurred backgrounds when users need to inspect the service.

## Governance

Every image records source, licence, release/consent, owner, permitted channels, expiration where applicable, crop variants, focal point, and alternative text. Named client or partner imagery requires documented approval.

## Performance

Produce responsive sizes and modern formats. Reserve layout dimensions before load. Use the smallest asset that remains sharp at the rendered size. Do not ship original camera files to clients.
