Photography

One real photograph sets the standard for the rest: a Xooter in the black polo with the violet cursive mark, at a front door, in daylight, mid-shot, at ease. Not stock, not staged mid-scrub. A site selling someone entry to their own home leads with a face, because the face is the argument.

Used full-bleed in the site hero, anchored at objectPosition: 50% 12% so the head survives a short viewport. It carries a generator watermark the hero frame crops — a stand-in until the shoot happens.
