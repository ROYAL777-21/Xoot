# Iconography

## System icons

Use the repository's established Lucide library on web. Use platform-native symbols or the approved Expo icon adapter on mobile. Match stroke weight, optical size, and baseline within a surface.

Use an icon-only button when a familiar symbol exists. Add an accessible name and a visible tooltip on pointer platforms for unfamiliar actions.

## Concierge mark

The Concierge mark is a simple robotic star: recognisable at launcher and notification sizes, symmetrical enough to remain stable while morphing, and distinct from a generic sparkle. It must be designed and approved as a Figma vector asset, exported through the asset pipeline, and recorded in the approved frame. Do not substitute emoji, ASCII, CSS art, or a hand-drawn production SVG.

## Rules

- Do not use letters as service icons.
- Do not mix filled, outline, and multicolour families without semantic reason.
- Status icons always include text.
- Do not animate icons unless the approved Figma motion explicitly does so.
- Sensitive actions use familiar, unambiguous symbols and confirmation copy.
- Icons do not imply a provider, partner, guarantee, or certification that Zoot cannot verify.
