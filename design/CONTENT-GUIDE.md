# Content Guide

## Voice

Calm, direct, warm, and practical. Prefer short sentences and concrete next actions. Avoid hype, defensiveness, internal terminology, and unsupported certainty.

## Product copy

- Use sentence case.
- Begin buttons with a clear verb: `Book a clean`, `Review booking`, `Get help`.
- State what happened, why it matters, and what the user can do next.
- Preserve user input after errors.
- Name pending states explicitly.
- Do not expose internal IDs, provider errors, fraud logic, or secrets.

## Trust and conversion

Describe verified process, approved benefits, and real limitations. Do not fabricate demand, scarcity, client relationships, ratings, guarantees, background-check outcomes, or response times.

## Concierge

Use direct answer, safe explanation, next action, then escalation where appropriate. If approved knowledge or safe account state is missing, say so rather than guessing.

## Safety and accessibility

Use plain language, specific labels, and instructions that do not depend on colour, position, or gesture alone. Emergency and urgent support copy follows approved market-specific policy; do not hard-code numbers without an owned source.

## Localisation

Store user-facing strings outside component logic where practical. Dates, time, currency, pluralisation, address formats, and phone formats must use locale-aware APIs.
