# Brand

## Product thesis

Soft surfaces. Clear decisions. Confident service.

Zoot helps people arrange trusted home and property services with less effort, while giving Zooters a clear, respectful way to work.

## Brand attributes

- Capable, never clinical
- Warm, never childish
- Premium through care and restraint, not decoration
- Direct, never abrupt
- Local and human, never fabricated
- Optimistic, never overpromising

## Name and voice

Render the brand as `Zoot`. Use sentence case in product copy. Prefer concrete verbs: book, review, track, message, rebook, get help. Do not use certainty language unless the backend verifies the state.

## Visual direction

The provisional owner preference is `A - Vibrant Native` with restrained editorial trust from direction C. This means light and energetic core surfaces, strong real-service imagery, crisp hierarchy, native interaction feedback, and limited clear glass. It is not approved until the relevant Figma frames are registered.

Care+ may use a deliberately dark premium surface. Do not apply that theme to the entire public or customer product.

## Trust rules

- Use real process evidence, verified reviews, and approved client proof only.
- Do not publish invented partners, logos, counts, testimonials, ratings, or guarantees.
- Explain screening, payment, support, and privacy in plain language.
- Use photographs that show the service and environment clearly.

## Existing assets

Current images and marks remain candidates. Each final asset needs an owner, licence or release source, crop specification, accessible alternative text, and a recorded Figma placement.
